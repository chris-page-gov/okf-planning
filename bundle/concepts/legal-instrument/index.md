# Legal Instrument

* [Article 4 direction](article-4-direction.md) - A local planning authority may create an [article 4 direction](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/when-is-permission-required#article-4-direction) to alter or remove [permitted development rights](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/permitted-development-rights-for-householders-technical-guidance) from a building or area.

Each direction may apply to one or more [article 4 direction areas](/dataset/article-4-direction-area), each with one or more [article 4 direction rules](/dataset/article-4-direction-rule).

This dataset contains data from [a small group of local planning authorities](/about/) who we are working with to develop a [data specification for article 4 directions](https://www.digital-land.info/guidance/specifications/article-4-direction).
* [development plan](development-plan.md) - This dataset is a package made from the local-plan, waste-plan, minerals-plan and supplementary-plan datasets.
* [Levelling-up and Regeneration Act 2023 (LURA)](levelling-up-and-regeneration-act-2023.md) - Statutory Act establishing National Development Management Policies (NDMPs), statutory local plan timetables, mandatory design codes, Infrastructure Levy, and environmental outcome reports.
* [Local plan](local-plan.md) - local plans prepared by a Local Planning Authority
* [local plan housing number](local-plan-housing.md) - Housing numbers for a Local Planning Authority in a local plan
* [minerals plan](minerals-plan.md) - minerals plans prepared by a Minerals and Waste Planning Authority
* [plan](plan.md) - This dataset is a package made from the local-plan, waste-plan, minerals-plan and supplementary-plan datasets.
* [supplementary plan](supplementary-plan.md) - supplementary local plans prepared by a Local Planning Authority
* [Town and Country Planning (Use Classes) Order 1987 (as amended)](use-classes-order-1987.md) - Statutory Instrument categorising the use of land and buildings (Class E Commercial, Class C3 Dwellinghouses, Class C4 HMOs, Class B2/B8 Industrial, Sui Generis).
* [Town and Country Planning Act 1990](town-and-country-planning-act-1990.md) - Primary Act of Parliament regulating the development of land in England and Wales, defining planning permission, planning obligations (Section 106), enforcement powers, and purchase notices.
* [Tree preservation order](tree-preservation-order.md) - This dataset contains information on trees with preservation orders.

It can be used for managing and protecting important trees by preventing their unauthorised removal or damage. 

Members of the public and developers use the data to check if a tree is protected and to inform planning applications or tree work proposals. The data helps ensure trees remain a significant part of the local environment and public amenity.
* [waste plan](waste-plan.md) - waste plans prepared by a Minerals and Waste Planning Authority
